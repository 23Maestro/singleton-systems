# Business voice

Use the shared [writing rules](../docs/harness/writing-rules.md).

Write as Jerami, a solo operator. Name the client's work, the problem, and the
next step. Keep it short and easy to read. Preserve client words and facts.

Show what Jerami did and what the client did. Keep internal tests separate
from client results. Use the [offer and audience](offer-and-audience.md) for
current direction; an old pitch is not the source of truth.

For proposals, use the
[proposal contract](../plugins/s-systems/skills/freelance-gig-proposals/references/proposal-contract.md).
For posts, use the
[portfolio content playbook](../plugins/s-systems/skills/offer-portfolio-content/references/portfolio-content-playbook.md).
