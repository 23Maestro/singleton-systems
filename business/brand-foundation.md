# Singleton Systems

Jerami runs Singleton Systems. He edits video and helps people fix work that
takes too many steps.

The common thread is the work itself: find what slows it down, learn how its
parts fit, and make a useful change that can be used again. Editing and workflow
cleanup both serve this goal.

Start from work already done. Find its source, owner, and result before making
a new plan or claiming a tool is missing. A past export proves that an export
was made. Client approval and a measured result need their own proof.
