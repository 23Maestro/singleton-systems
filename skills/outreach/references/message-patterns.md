# Messages

## Choose 1 next action

Use 1 useful angle, checked facts and 1 CTA. A CTA can ask to receive work,
answer a question, name an owner or speak. A direct offer need not ask for a
meeting. Match the ask to the real conversation and what Jerami can deliver.
Use plain words and an accurate subject. Use Re or Fwd only for a real reply
or forward. Keep links and formatting minimal where they add no value.

## Finished sample offer

Jerami's former Gift style is a useful option: name the work reviewed, explain
one useful change, say what is already made, then ask permission to share it.
Keep it short. About 50–65 words is a drafting aid, not a validity test.
A first permission note can omit links, pricing and meeting asks. Those are
choices for that offer, not rules for all outreach or claims about spam.

Example structure, not a claim that this work exists:

> I watched [checked piece]. [Specific part] could help [buyer task].
> I made [finished sample and actual change]. Okay if I send it over?

If the work is not made, offer to prepare it with truthful scope and timing.
Do not call an unfinished MVP a finished sample or a paid client result.

## Direct offer

Name the useful service or result and why it fits this buyer. Use supportable
proof or a clear mechanism when available. Ask for the next useful step.
Pricing can fit a requested quote or accepted offer. Do not hide it behind a
sample rule once the buyer asks.

> We [actual service] for [verified use]. Want [useful options or quote inputs]?

## Waiting for an answer

Read full history first. Choose a short bump, a useful finding, relevant proof
or a routing question. Keep the next action tied to the accepted offer.
A month-old conversation can warrant a planned follow-up even after 2 earlier
sends. Do not resend the same pitch on a timer or invent new research.
Use the date guide for the selected treatment.

## After engagement

A yes to the sample means deliver the promised sample. Check that its link
opens and plays on a phone. Do not require a meeting to receive it. Keep
third-party work within the agreed review scope; publication needs its own
permission. The next question may concern the next unit of work if it fits.

An interest question about useful paid work is valid when it fits the buyer.
It does not fail just because a sample could have been offered instead.
Answer the actual question, objection or booking step before adding a pitch.
Do not use a cold timer after a human reply.

## Check the draft

Check every rendered name, fact, link, number and promise. Show 1 draft for the
chosen next action. Rejected drafts are not approved examples. Preserve
Jerami's edits. If he rejects the point, fix the point before polishing words.
Never invent views, lost revenue, relationships, referrals or finished work.
