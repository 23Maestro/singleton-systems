# Fit and sourcing

## Choose the buyer's task

Read the Lane's current offer, proof and delivery limits. Define the company,
role, useful result and next conversation. Keep observed facts, confirmed
business facts and assumptions separate. A durable problem can support an
offer. A fresh company event is useful timing evidence, not a required excuse.
Hiring alone does not prove a pipeline problem.

Choose a direct offer when the buyer can judge its value. Offer an asset when
it helps them make a real decision. A framework, bounded audit, sample, quote
or call may fit. Say what exists, what needs work, its inputs and delivery time.
An asset must be useful without a hidden meeting or purchase condition.
Promise only work the business can fulfill.

## Improve the existing list first

Read the current sheet, field notes, Gmail and accepted next actions. Split:
uncontacted fit accounts, pending follow-ups, engaged people and suppressed
or unverified records. Fix missing next actions before buying more records.
Skip people Jerami excludes. Do not infer a follow-up failure from a send count
alone; check when the sends happened and the later conversation.

For a new source, set a small sample and budget before collection. Qualify
accounts before paying for contact enrichment. Keep stable company IDs,
source URLs, dates, fit reasons, unknowns and duplicate checks.
A public directory shows membership or fit; it does not prove buying intent
or permission to introduce anyone.

## Use the smallest tool stack

| Job | First choice | Add a tool when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recover contact and exposure | Existing sheet and Gmail | The current record lacks a needed fact |
| Find fit accounts | Relevant directories, company sites or existing search | A sample shows a real coverage gap |
| Find a role and work email | Existing data, then one enrichment provider | The account fits and the role is useful |
| Check addresses | Existing verifier | A current address check is needed |
| Plan and track | Existing sheet and Asana | The chosen action needs a missing field |

Exa, Hunter, ZeroBounce and a Composio wallet are optional routes for those
jobs. The supplied video shows that stack. Its reported 20 contacts for $0.68
is demo tool spend, not a verified cost per qualified lead for this list.
It does not establish current prices, model cost, coverage or reply quality.
Check current tools and account access before recommending a paid change.
Test accepted contacts, duplicate rate, verified usable coverage and total cost
per qualified usable contact. Do not buy subscriptions or migrate records just
to match a demo.

Keep valid, invalid, unknown and catch-all labels with verification dates.
Hold unknown and catch-all addresses unless the sending owner has an accepted
policy. Apply contact and account exclusions before final list review.

## Plan coverage before scale

Count reachable accounts, people and messages separately. Choose coverage,
sequence and capacity together. Keep space for follow-ups and replies.
Daily sends derive from planned touches and eligible days; mailbox supply
does not establish demand or delivery health.

For a new or scaled campaign, record sender identity, authentication, monitored
reply route, eligible calendar, capacity, stop rules and fulfillment owner.
Use dedicated infrastructure under the accepted campaign plan. Do not change
providers or domains as part of a routine existing-thread review.

Judge small cohorts by actual account evidence and interested replies.
A small existing list cannot prove a winning tool or cadence. Diagnose history
and exposure, delivery and data, fit, offer, then copy before scaling.
