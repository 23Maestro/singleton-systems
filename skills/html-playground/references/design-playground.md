# Design Playground Lane

Use for components, layouts, spacing, color, typography, motion, or responsive
behavior.

- Controls: sliders for scale, spacing, and radius; toggles for optional
  behavior; selectors or cards for discrete layout choices.
- Preview: render the component or screen fragment in context and update its
  styles directly from state.
- Prompt: translate numbers into qualitative direction and include exact CSS
  values only where they help implementation.
- Presets: offer cohesive directions such as Balanced, Compact, Editorial, or
  High Contrast rather than isolated parameter changes.

Show light and dark context when it affects the decision. Keep advanced
controls collapsed until they are useful.
