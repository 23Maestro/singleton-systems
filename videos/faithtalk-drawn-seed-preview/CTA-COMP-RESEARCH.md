## Recommended adaptation — design judgment

Use the supplied sports-stat example's readable dark plate, generous padding and thin edge. Recolor the cyan edge with approved FaithTalk emerald or muted gold, set main copy in warm white, and keep rounding subtle. Avoid overdone glass distortion, bright neon glow, sporty compressed type, or a full-frame color wash.

Two related scales:

- Compact host/guest lower third: FaithTalk emblem, fine gold separator, name, smaller role; panel stays below faces.
- Larger outro card: one action headline, one supporting sentence, one dominant destination. Keep GLTV smaller in a consistent corner.

CTA fields:

- Connect: Stay Connected / social handle + landing page.
- Prayer: Need Prayer? / confirmed prayer number and online request option.
- Story: Let Your Faith Talk / Share your story for a chance to be featured + confirmed submission destination.
- Next week: Coming Up Next Week / replaceable guest/topic + supplied episode clip.

QR codes are optional additions only after the destination is confirmed. Do not make a QR the only route. All contacts, handles, next-week clips and details remain editable.

Motion recommendation: one quiet panel reveal, brief stagger between heading and detail, then a readable hold. Match timing to the approved VO. The larger CTA panel does not require replicating the seed animation.
