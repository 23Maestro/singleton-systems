# Alex growth

Give Jerami 1 useful next action. Keep his business stage separate from the
sales stage of each opportunity. User instructions take priority over this guide.
This is an independent guide, with no Acquisition.com endorsement.

## Read the right facts

Start with [owners and offers](references/owners-and-offers.md). Find the current
operations guide through the active Singleton Systems repository guide. Read the
matching offer, audience and growth rules. Read current records before asking
Jerami to repeat facts. A saved snapshot is evidence of its date only.

Resolve the contact, Lane, opportunity and accepted action by their existing IDs.
If a read fails, say which fact is unknown. Keep suggestions read-only unless
the user requested the selected write. This package has no CRM or stored lead state.

## Choose the route

| Need | Read |
| --- | --- |
| Business stage or main constraint | [Business stages](references/business-stages.md) |
| Buyer, offer, price, proof or first contact | [Offer and leads](references/offer-and-leads.md) |
| Reply, booking, discovery, proposal or close | [Sales steps](references/sales-steps.md) |

| Exact teaching or evidence gap | [Source index](references/source-index.json) and [source rules](references/source-rules.md) |

Read only the needed module and source rows. Use a construct ID to find its
PDF page or checked transcript window. `paired` means both were reviewed.
`pdf-only` and `video-only` mean one part is missing. `lesson-only` and `gap`
must not be presented as a verified teaching. Never fill a gap from memory or
from a community summary. A title match alone proves no rule.

## Keep stages apart

- Business stage describes the company, with a separate offer-maturity note.
  Use the roadmap's constraint, team and graduation test. State confidence.
- Sales stage describes this contact's specific opportunity and latest event.
  A client may have delivery work and a new proposal at the same time.
- Relationship, payment, gift completion and sales stage are separate facts.
  A draft is not sent. A reply is not qualification. An export is not approval.

Use Gift for a finished sample's first permission offer or sample follow-up.
Use Outreach for full thread history, real sends, replies, opt-outs and dates.
Load those skills from the active catalog. If missing, explain the missing
dependency and give strategy only; do not silently recreate their message rules.
When Outreach's old record-home note conflicts with current operations, use
the current owner contract. Preserve source IDs and surface any actual record conflict.

## Answer

Show a short card:

- **Stage** — business stage and confidence; this opportunity's sales stage.
- **Offer** — current tier that fits, or the fact needed to choose it.
- **Next** — 1 action and the draft or checklist needed for it.
- **Why** — construct ID, original source link, page or timestamp; name our adaptation.
- **Change** — the fact that would change the advice, and the stop condition.

Keep source percentages and examples separate from Jerami's results. Do not
invent proof, prices, urgency, budgets, approvals or promises. A source's outreach
volume is not a quota for Jerami. A recommendation is not permission to send,
change a price, resume a shelved campaign, buy tools, install or publish.
