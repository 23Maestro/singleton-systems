# Keep and grow

Use these service adaptations only after checking the current work and agreement.
The named paid Retention and Lifetime Value playbooks remain missing originals.
The public retention video supplies the checked teaching below.

## Deliver and renew

Use `first-win` and `activation` to make the first result clear. For editing,
confirm footage, style and the first review. For consulting, run the small fix
on a real example and hand over steps. Keep export, client approval, delivery
and payment as separate events. Use the existing client-work record.

Use `cancel-reasons`, `stay-reasons`, `reduce-overwhelm` and `simple-path` to
learn what helps clients. Ask why work continues or ends. Keep a small number
of cases as observations; do not claim a statistical trend. Skool membership
benchmarks are not editing or consulting retention benchmarks.

Before renewal, review the promise, delivered work, unresolved issues and next
need. Offer a next period only when it fits. One seasonal client waiting for
more footage is not automatically churn. The renewal checklist is our adaptation;
no dedicated renewal playbook was recovered.

## Expand

Use `second-offer` and `menu-upsell`: find the next real problem, then show which
current option fits. Editing volume can justify moving from 4 + 2 to 8 + 4.
Consulting upkeep can justify an ongoing tier after a working fix. Neither move
is automatic. Do not sell a second service to hide poor delivery of the first.

Use `scope-downsell` and `tier-evidence` when good-fit buyers need less scope.
Do not slash price while keeping all the work. The detailed classic, anchor and
rollover upsells, free trials, continuity bonus and waived-fee plays remain source
gaps. Read their original lessons before claiming specific rules.

`annual-plan` is a public community example. It does not authorize annual terms
for Jerami. Check demand, support cost and agreement before proposing continuity.
Use the actual offer's monthly or one-time terms until changed by the user.

## Refer and return

Use `referral` after a useful result or appropriate engaged conversation. Ask
for a relevant introduction without implying it already exists. An introduction
is not permission to use the referrer's name in every pitch.

Use `reactivation` for a real prior need with a checked new reason or agreed date.
Read opt-outs, declines and later replies first. Do not turn an old gift topic
into a current event. Preserve the old opportunity and create no new record
unless the owner workflow calls for it and the user requested the write.

Use `price-change` to draft a clear account of current value and a proposed
change. Keep the full price, effective date, scope and options clear. The video's
business results are Alex's examples. They do not predict Jerami's revenue.
