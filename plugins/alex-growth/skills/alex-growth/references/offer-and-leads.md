# Offer and leads

Use the source index IDs below. The actions in this file are our service
adaptations. The linked originals support the underlying ideas.

## Pick a buyer

Use `market-fit` and `best-customer`. Review the buyer's problem, ability to
pay, reachability and market. For existing work, compare who stays, pays and
fits the service. Do not use sensitive personal traits as sales filters.

## Build the offer

Use `dream-outcome`, `proof`, `time-to-value`, `buyer-effort`, `offer-components`,
`delivery-cube` and `trim-stack`. Map the buyer's goal to the smallest useful
delivery. Show what the buyer supplies, what Jerami does and what approval ends
the work. Choose a current tier before inventing a new package.

For editing, useful proof is a relevant finished edit with Jerami's role clear.
For consulting, a tested internal example can show a method. It must not be
presented as a paid client result. Promise an output or test we can control;
do not promise views, revenue or time savings without evidence.

`bonuses`, `guarantees`, `scarcity`, `urgency` and `naming` are offer tools.
They are not defaults. Keep any bonus bounded. Use real capacity and dates.
Do not add refunds, performance promises or free service until the terms are
accepted. Use clear names and the current offer's actual scope.

## Get leads

Use `core-four` to choose one channel from warm contact, cold contact, content
or ads. Use the current Lane's policy and capacity. Do not import Alex's daily
volume or spend targets into Jerami's calendar. Compare real responses and
qualified work before changing channels.

Use `lead-magnet` for a complete first result on a narrow problem. A gift is
useful only when it fits this buyer. Use `prepared-sample` and Gift when a
finished sample is ready. If unfinished, the next step is to finish and check
the agreed sample, or choose another honest form of first contact. Never say
“I made this” before the work exists. Do not add unpaid work without a bound.

## Start contact

Use `warm-reply`, `aca`, `goal-question` and `same-thread`. Read history through
Outreach before drafting. A mutual connection does not prove a referral. A
person following an account does not prove buying interest. Keep one ask tied
to a checked fact.

Cold silence, a requested sample and an engaged lead are different states.
Gift's permission-first copy and Outreach's send limits govern their work.
Do not overwrite them with a longer source sequence. Opt-outs and clear declines
stop outreach. No response does not prove delivery, reading or rejection.
