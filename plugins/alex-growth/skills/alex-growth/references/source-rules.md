# Source rules

The index holds short paraphrases and original links. Full PDFs and captions
stay outside the plugin. No books or paid playbooks are bundled.

## Status

| Status | What it proves |
| --- | --- |
| paired | The named PDF pages and transcript window were read for this idea. |
| pdf-only | The named PDF pages were read. A matching video window is open. |
| video-only | The timestamped captions were read. A matching original PDF is missing. |
| lesson-only | An official lesson exists. Its teaching has not been checked. |
| gap | No original evidence was read for this idea. |

Caption review is text review, not a claim that the video was watched. Captions
can have errors. Check audio before quoting unclear words. Index page numbers
are 1-based PDF file pages; the printed page can differ. Only the listed pages
are claimed as reviewed. Recovery and text extraction do not mean full reading.

The live ScrapeCreators YouTube transcript spec permits any video length when
public captions exist. It uses no AI fallback. This overrides the saved skill's
older 2-minute note for that endpoint. One upsell video returned null captions;
it remains a gap. The official lesson pages use Mux. Their checked playlists
had no subtitle track. A working player alone is not transcript evidence.

## Missing originals

See `missingOriginals` in the index. The named paid playbooks came from a
community manifest. Their full contents and constructs remain unknown here.
The public videos support their own ideas; they do not prove coverage of those
books. Never call this package the complete paid library.

Stage 0 has a live official lesson, but its guessed PDF returned 404 in prior
research. The Stage 1 PDF discusses the preceding free-work phase; this does
not replace a Stage 0 original. Several later money-model lessons are listed
by the publisher, but their individual teaching remains open.

The 2 community repositories were inspected for organization and missing titles.
Neither was installed. Their private paths and extracted books were not copied.

## Advice boundary

Use checked teaching for strategy. Label the service-specific application as
our adaptation. A PDF-only rule can be used with its video gap shown. For a
gap, use current business rules or ask for evidence; do not attribute an invented
rule to Alex. Source examples do not authorize their promises or tactics for us.

Do not carry over fake scarcity, implied referrals, pressure, hidden fees or
claims that the source business earned. Use a clear decline as a stop. Show full
price and terms. Ask only for work facts needed to choose the next step.
