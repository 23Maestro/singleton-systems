# Sales steps

These are our workflow steps. They are not the scaling roadmap. Each next
action needs a real event and the current offer. Source IDs refer to the index.

| State | Next useful action | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research | Check buyer fit and the problem. | market-fit, best-customer |
| Ready for first contact | Confirm the sample or offer is true; load Gift or Outreach. | prepared-sample, goal-question |
| Cold email unanswered | Read later replies and total sends; let Outreach set the review. | same-thread |
| Human reply | Answer their point, then ask what drew their interest. | warm-reply, aca |
| Qualification | Confirm need, decision process, budget and timing. | qualify |
| Booking | Agree on a real time and who needs to attend. | booking, decision-maker |
| No-show | Check the calendar and thread; ask about the missed step. | booking, meeting-next |
| Discovery | Learn the goal, present state and prior attempts. | clarify, label-gap, past-attempts |
| Proposal | Map the agreed need to scope, proof, price and terms. | outcome-pitch |
| Objection | Ask which concern remains, then answer it with facts. | obstacles, decision-maker |
| Decision | Confirm acceptance and the exact terms. | close-agreement |
| Follow-up | Return to the agreed step on its real date. | meeting-next, same-thread |
| Delivery | Confirm inputs, owner, first review and finish check. | first-win |

## Calls

Use `pre-call` to read their notes first. Use CLOSER as a learning aid for a
transactional call. Its checked components here are clarify, label, overview
past attempts, outcome pitch and concerns. Reinforcement has a separate source
gap; onboarding advice uses the checked retention video. Do not silently call
an incomplete source map the full Closing playbook.

Ask useful work questions without amplifying distress or shaming a buyer.
For editing: which videos, source length, style, cadence, approver and deadline?
For consulting: which repeated task, examples, tools, access, errors and test?
Ask about decision makers directly. Do not pressure someone to bypass a partner.
Only ask for budget when it helps qualify the stated work; unknown stays unknown.

## Proposal and close

The proposal checklist is our adaptation: goal, deliverables, exclusions,
client inputs, approvals, dates, price, payment and the next decision. Check
the current agreement and public tier first. No source supplies Jerami's custom
price. An email saying “sounds good” may still leave scope or payment open.

Use `terms-downsell` or `scope-downsell` when the real issue fits. Changing terms
or removing work changes the offer; it is not a hidden discount. Obtain approval
for new terms before quoting them. Respect a no, and leave a poor fit closed.

## Follow-up

Outreach owns event counts and date math. A promised date takes priority.
The cold default remains first contact plus 1 nudge, then park. A warm lead's
agreed step does not use the cold timer. A new subject does not reset send count.
Proposal silence is not a new cold lead. Do not restart a parked lead merely
because the source mentions a 3–6 month cadence. Use `reactivation` with a
checked new reason and the user's choice to contact them again.
