# Keep the choices distinct

Gary Vaynerchuk's own explanation: https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-one-thing-i-didnt-clarify-enough-in-jab-jab-jab-right-hook/

- Jab: give the viewer useful value. No sales ask is required.
- Hook / right hook: make a direct ask, such as hiring, subscribing or buying.
- Feint: Gary groups this with jab content as free, useful information that helps the audience. This source does not give jab and feint separate editorial definitions. Keep both labels in the give family; do not assign personal stories only to feint without a choice from Jerami.

Current source: [Gary’s 2026 breakdown](https://garyvee.substack.com/p/the-new-rules-of-social-media-2026), July 2, under “You Just Suck at It.” He explains that repeated sales pitches become predictable. His earlier clarification also says that giving value does not entitle the creator to a sale.

The boxing choice is the post's purpose. The 8 story lanes choose what part of Jerami's story supplies the idea. A format is the container: Reel, carousel, image, text or Story. An opening hook is the first spoken or on-screen line. The Instagram pack's 26 opening formulas belong to this last choice. Creator references inform presentation and structure, not Jerami's facts or a copied voice.
