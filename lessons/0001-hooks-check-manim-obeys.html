The dumb-simple answer: Manim will not make Figma smarter. It gives Codex a clock that is easier to program, rerun, inspect, and correct.

Manim = robot stagehand

Give it 1.550 seconds. It repeats 1.550 seconds.

Your hook can prove that a number is present and consistent. It cannot decide whether that number hits the best spoken beat.

How it fits your current workflow

Premiere transcript→ Cue map→ Hook checks→ Figma design→ Figma or Manim render→ Frame proof→ Premiere placement

Your recent pre-hook blocks a Figma mutation when required skills are missing. The timing checks require a verified transcript anchor, entrance times, a five-second final hold, and exact Premiere placement. Those checks protect the contract. Manim executes the contract with code.
