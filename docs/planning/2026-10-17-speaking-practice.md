# Speaking practice

Source: Jerami’s attached video, `1-r07qxo-R-Download.mp4` (57.92 seconds). Notes checked against its speech and on-screen steps.

## The exercise

1. Ask AI for 20 questions that help it get to know you deeply.
2. Record yourself answering them for 20 minutes. Do not rehearse.
3. Leave the recording alone for 24 hours.
4. Listen without watching. Note your speaking speed and what you hear in your voice.
5. Watch with the sound off. Note what you see in your face and body.
6. Read the transcript. Ask AI to find words that add no meaning to your message.

The speaker says this will reveal 20–30 things to work on. We do not need to force that count.

## Our first test

Use the questions below in order. Aim for about 1 minute each. Keep recording through pauses and mistakes.

After the 24-hour wait, do each review on its own. Save a timecode with each note. Pick 2 habits to work on. Keep the first recording as the baseline.

## Questions for the recording

1. How would you describe yourself without naming your job?
2. What matters most to you right now, and why?
3. When do you feel most like yourself?
4. What experience changed how you see yourself?
5. What are you proud of that people rarely see?
6. What do people often misunderstand about you?
7. What makes a good day for you?
8. What drains you, even when you do it well?
9. What do you enjoy enough to lose track of time?
10. What belief have you changed your mind about?
11. What do you do when you feel stuck?
12. What fear shapes your choices most?
13. What hard choice taught you something useful?
14. What do you want people to feel after talking with you?
15. When is it hardest to say what you mean?
16. What do you wish you could explain more clearly?
17. What pattern in your life do you want to change?
18. What does enough money mean for your daily life?
19. What would you try if you trusted yourself more?
20. What do you want your life to look like in 1 year?

## Prompt for the transcript review

Read this transcript of my unrehearsed speech. Find filler words, empty phrases, and repeats that add no meaning. Quote the exact examples and keep their timecodes when present. Keep words that carry meaning or show my voice. Give me 2 habits to practice first. Do not infer my tone or body language from text alone.
