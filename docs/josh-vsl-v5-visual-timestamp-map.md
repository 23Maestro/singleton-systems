## How the timestamps were derived

The document gives section-level ranges only. Every sub-beat timestamp in this map is
**ESTIMATED**: within each section, the section's stated start and end are divided
across its sentences in proportion to spoken word count, then the phrase is placed at
its cumulative position. All 11 stated ranges tile exactly to 1200 s with no gaps.

Treat these as edit-planning anchors, not audio-frame timings. Conform against the
recorded VO before cutting.

ranges — it runs from 1.94 words/sec (Section 8) to 3.18 words/sec (Section 1), a 64%
spread. Sections 1, 9 and 11 are the fastest and therefore the least reliable;
estimates inside them can drift several seconds. Sections 3, 5, 6 and 8 sit near the
document's overall 2.45 w/s average and are the most trustworthy.
