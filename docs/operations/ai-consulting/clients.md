# Consulting clients

Use the shared [client card and follow-up rules](../clients-and-outreach.md).

[Open Supabase](https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/elsvolwngolywlmgitmd/editor)
and [Asana AI Consulting](https://app.asana.com/0/1218884867598641/list).

The real client and prospect records have not been moved into `contacts`.
[Reconcile client records](https://linear.app/23maestro/issue/23M-407) owns that
work, with links to its evidence. Do not present a test row as a live client list.

Use the same 4 views as [editing](../content-editor/clients.md): current work,
returning or seasonal clients, new prospects and unpaid collaborations.

Intent Solutions is an unpaid collaboration. Keep its contacts and opportunities
linked to the same relationship even when the work spans editing, content and
consulting. Confirm contact spelling and roles from the source before creating records.
