# Editing growth

The goal is more paying clients. Lead with agency outreach and suitable contract
roles. Choose the next route from real fit and the path to paid work. Check current
clients for extra work or referrals alongside new leads.

Keep other good opportunities open. A new priority does not remove an earlier
lead. Use its history and next step to decide when to act.

Use editing examples, social posts and SEO to support the client search. Keep
that work moving without making it a gate before outreach or applications.
Keep this work in [Asana Content Editor](https://app.asana.com/0/1218890014545436/list),
even when an agent writes it.

## Cost to win work

A short mock, tailored reel or free test edit can be part of the cost to win a
client. Use the [outreach flow](outreach.md) to choose and bound that work.
Record prep time and direct tool or asset costs with the chosen task. Check the
likely paid work and whether the sample can be reused before spending more.

Track interested replies, paid projects and repeat work. Income and lead-count
goals guide effort; they do not prove demand, set prices or promise a result.
