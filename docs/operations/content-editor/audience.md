# Editing audience

## Lead with agencies

Start with creative agencies and production teams that sell video work and need
outside editing or motion help. Contract hiring and requests for help with client
work are useful signals. Check that the need is current and the work fits.

An in-house team can still need help. A studio selling editing does not by itself
show a need to hire Jerami.

## Keep other routes open

Creators and local businesses can still fit when they have useful paid work to
hand off. Keep good earlier leads available. Give each one its next step or hold.
Honor named skips, closed opportunities and requests to stop.
Sports highlight work serves athletes and their families.

Start local discovery in Tampa Bay. Pursue remote roles Jerami selects when
they fit. Broad national prospecting stays on hold. Keep serving current clients
outside the area.
