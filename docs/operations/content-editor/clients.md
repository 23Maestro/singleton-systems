## Sharing work

Jerami confirmed the FaithTalk / 745 Creative NDA on November 20, 2026.
Keep it out of reels, application samples and sales assets unless the
agreement or written permission allows that exact use. A client posting the
work does not grant portfolio permission.

For any client, check the asset and its allowed use before sharing. When useful
work cannot be shown, use a self-directed mock made from your own or permitted
material. Show the same skill without copying private client files, brand assets
or data. The [outreach flow](outreach.md#choose-the-proof) owns the proof choice.
