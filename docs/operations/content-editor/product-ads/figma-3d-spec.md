# Figma 3D transforms spec

A 15 s, 9:16 spec ad for Figma's new 3D transforms. It is built in Figma with that same tool.

Reference: Figma's launch reel, 35 s, 16:9. https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfpjrYsvUpy/
It has 3D type ribbons, card stacks, posters that turn, a type cube and the Figma editor.
We keep the idea of flat things turning in space. Every frame is our own design.

## Beats

| Time | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 s | Black screen. 1 flat white card says "Your designs are flat." |
| 2 to 4 s | The card tips back. A big "X" stamps on. |
| 4 to 6 s | It turns sideways. "Y" stamps on. |
| 6 to 8 s | It spins. "Z" stamps on. 6 more cards fan out into a ring. |
| 8 to 11 s | A ribbon of words, "depth, angle, space", wraps around a turning type cube. |
| 11 to 13 s | Pull back to a Figma-style panel with the 3 turn sliders moving. |
| 13 to 15 s | End card: "3D transforms. Now in Figma." Small line: "Spec ad. Not affiliated with Figma." |

Motion moves on 2s. Colors: black, lime, violet and coral.

## Build

1. Design the cards and type in Figma.
2. Turn them with Figma 3D transforms.
3. Animate them with Figma motion keyframes.
4. Export the video from Figma. Add music and sound with ffmpeg.
