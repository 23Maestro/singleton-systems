## How to read this

- Length comes from the ad's video data.
- Meta and Google do not return the frame shape. Meta ads here run on Facebook, Instagram and Threads feeds and Reels. Open each one to confirm 9:16 or 4:5.
- Instagram reels are 9:16. YouTube uploads are 16:9.
- The motion note comes from the ad copy, title and length. Confirm it on the first watch.
- Creator talking-head ads are left out unless the motion looks strong.

7. **Olipop Sparkle mascot** ([DgAnGgIAFMZ](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgAnGgIAFMZ/), [behind the scenes](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgGFR3fDTJO/)). A handmade character next to the can. Remix as a 3D or stop-motion mascot.
