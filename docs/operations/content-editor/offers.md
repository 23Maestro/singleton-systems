# Editing services

Video editing and motion graphics lead today. Recurring weekly edits are current
income work. Sports highlights are occasional work.

## Agency offer

<!-- writing-source:start -->
> I help creative agencies handle video editing and motion graphics when their team is stretched. We agree on the scope and deadline, then start with 1 paid project.
<!-- writing-source:end -->

Lead new business with this offer. Take useful work from other buyers when it
fits. A project can lead to repeat work; a monthly retainer is optional.
Income goals guide the client search. Price each job from its scope and terms.

## First step

Use the [outreach flow](outreach.md) to choose the next step. A relevant work
sample may be enough. A short mock or free test edit can help earn the first
paid job when proof is missing. Set its scope and time limit before making it.

## Scope and proof

Confirm scope, price and delivery from the current client agreement before quoting.
The public homepage presents the broader editing offer. Recruiting highlights
are a focused offer within Content Editor, with their own page and intake.
Do not replace working intake forms during a docs move.

Use relevant work that can be shared. Follow the [client proof limits](clients.md#sharing-work).
Keep client approval separate from an export. Label self-directed mock work clearly.
