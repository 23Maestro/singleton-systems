# Editing outreach

Use the shared [client card and follow-up rules](../clients-and-outreach.md).

## Choose the opportunity

Lead with the [agency offer](offers.md#agency-offer) and [buyer screen](audience.md).
Check contract roles and requests for outside help. Read the brief, required
tools, work hours and hiring steps. Match the pitch to the actual work.

Prioritize a good path to paid work. Current clients and referrals remain useful
alongside applications and new leads. Keep other good buyers open with their own
next step. A new focus does not close or reset earlier outreach.
Keep seasonal follow-ups tied to real season dates. Do not treat a past client
as a cold lead.

## Choose the proof

Start with relevant work that can be shared under the [client proof limits](clients.md#sharing-work).

When public proof is thin, a short mock or tailored reel can show the needed
skill. Use your own or permitted source material. Label it as mock work
and name what you did. It can show craft without client campaign results.

A free test edit can also help a buyer judge fit. Choose the one output, source
material and time limit before starting. This work is a cost to win the client.
It is optional; do not require a new sample for every lead or turn it into a
full unpaid project. Keep unfinished work described as unfinished.

## Choose the next step

When the proof answers the buyer's question, ask for one clear next step. A
contract client can start with 1 paid project at an agreed scope and deadline.
An application follows that role's hiring steps and work terms.

When fit or the need is unclear, ask the missing question or hold that lead.
Continue other suitable opportunities. Use [growth](growth.md) to weigh sample
effort against the possible paid work.

## Keep the record

The [outreach sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1RI6OGmv9HF5NTmHJu4Vk6QInT3Fs5-lOeXUZPTp8y28/edit)
keeps history. Asana holds the next action. Both must link to the same Supabase
client or prospect once the [record move](clients.md) is complete.

Use the shared [Outreach guide](../../../skills/outreach/SKILL.md). Gift opens
the same sample guidance. Gmail supplies message facts. Read
later replies before drafting. Sending needs approval for the actual message.
Do not create a second outreach ledger.
