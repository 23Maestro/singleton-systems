# Future Voices — 30-second social brief

## Goal

Turn Stuart's visually damaged first interview into a short that introduces
Future Voices on Facebook and LinkedIn. Michelle's audio carries the story.
Stuart's voice connects her experience to the series.

This is a Future Voices brand ad. It is not a trailer for the full recording.
The hook must work without sound. Michelle appears only in verified live
frames because her feed freezes.

## Delivery

- Facebook: 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 29.97 fps.
- LinkedIn: 1080 × 1350, 4:5 reframe.
- Runtime: 29–30 seconds.
- Burned-in phrase captions inside the shared 4:5 safe area.
- CTA: `FOLLOW THE CONVERSATION` plus the approved handle or URL.

Build the 9:16 sample first. Keep all text, faces, and logos inside the center
4:5 crop. Reframe only after Stuart approves the cut.

motion. No fake product UI. No stock montage. The 1600 work guides spacing and
timing only. The YouTube Short guides chapter rhythm only; none of its “TIPS”
content enters this edit.
