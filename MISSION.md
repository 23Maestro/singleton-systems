# Mission: Transcript-timed motion

## Why

Build client motion scenes that land on the spoken beat with frame-level control. Keep the approved Figma design system and the existing Lineups review gates.

## Success looks like

- Explain what the hook, cue map, renderer, and Premiere each own.
- Turn transcript timestamps into scene-relative cue times.
- Choose Figma Motion or Manim based on the scene.
- Check the rendered frame at every important cue.

## Constraints

- Two client assignments arrive on September 2, 2026.
- Figma stays the approved visual source.
- Jerami approves the scene before any Premiere placement or Eagle write.
- Teaching uses plain language and one real Lineups example at a time.

## Out of scope

- Replacing Figma, Premiere, or Eagle.
- Porting every motion scene to Manim.
- Changing hooks or model settings during this lesson.
